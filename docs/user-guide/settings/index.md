# Settings

The Grid panel's **Settings** view is where you tune the device's behaviour after the basics are in place. Each section is covered on its own page:

- **[Entities](entities.md)** — enable, disable, and rename individual Home Assistant entities.
- **[Detection](detection.md)** — per-sensor ranges and sensitivity.
- **[Reporting](reporting.md)** — how often the device publishes targets and zone state.
- **[Environmental](environmental.md)** — offsets for the temperature and humidity readings.
- **[LED and relay](led-relay.md)** — LED modes, colours, brightness; relay trigger mode and contact mode.
- **[Logging](logging.md)** — per-component log levels for firmware and integration.

!!! note
    Settings are stored on the device keyed by MAC address, so they follow a device through re-flashes and firmware upgrades.
